Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the concerns patients usually want clarified first.

Practical questions often decide whether someone books or leaves. We keep these answers simple and visible.

What is the first step if I am not sure which treatment I need?

Start with a free consultation so the clinic can understand your condition, explain suitable options and advise what should happen next.

Are braces and clear aligners both available?

Yes. Anna Dental Clinics offers conventional braces, Damon self-ligating braces and clear aligners, depending on suitability and patient preference.

Do you provide care for families too?

Yes. General dentistry remains an important part of the clinic experience, which makes the brand suitable for parents, children and older family members.

Can final pricing be confirmed online?

No. The figures shown on the site are indicative ranges only. Final pricing depends on your dental condition, treatment complexity and clinical assessment.

Which locations are currently shown on the page?

The current locations listed are Subang Jaya, Bayan Lepas, George Town, Taman Molek and Pelangi.
